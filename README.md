# My Favorite Series: The Mentalist

## About the Series
*The Mentalist* is a crime drama series about Patrick Jane, a former psychic who uses his excellent observation skills to help the California Bureau of Investigation solve crimes.

## Main Characters
- **Patrick Jane** - A consultant who notices details that others often miss.
- **Teresa Lisbon** - The team leader who works closely with Jane.
- **Kimball Cho** - A serious and observant member of the team.
- **Wayne Rigsby** - An agent who helps investigate different cases.
- **Grace Van Pelt** - A team member who is skilled in research and technology.

## My Favorite Character
My favorite character is **Teresa Lisbon** because she is responsible, strong, and dedicated to her work. I like how she stays professional while handling difficult cases and how she looks after her team.

## Why I Like It
I enjoy this series because I like mysteries and trying to figure out the truth along with the characters. The combination of clever investigations and interesting characters makes it enjoyable to watch.

## My Favorite Part
1. Watching Jane solve difficult cases.
2. Seeing Lisbon lead and protect her team.
3. Discovering the clues behind each mystery.

> A good investigator notices what others overlook.
